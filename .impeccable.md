## Design Context

### Users

CISS guards use the mobile landing page outdoors and on duty to mark attendance
quickly with a phone number or employee QR. New guards use the same entry point
to reach enrollment. Field officers, clients, and administrators primarily use
the wider application after staff sign-in.

### Brand Personality

Dependable, disciplined, and distinctly Indian. The experience should feel like
an official CISS operational product: established, security-focused, and clear
under pressure.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the official CISS deep navy and restrained gold identity with the CISS logo,
strong typography, and clear operational language. Support both light and dark
device themes. Mobile should be concise and touch-first; desktop can reveal
more company and platform context.

### Design Principles

1. Attendance is always the first and clearest mobile action.
2. Show “CISS Workforce” and official ownership without marketing clutter.
3. Keep guard login and enrollment visible but secondary to attendance.
4. Put staff access and corporate links at the bottom on mobile.
5. Use minimum 44px touch targets, plain language, and strong contrast.
